Duskmoth docs

Everything about how Duskmoth moves SOL and USDC privately on Solana mainnet: the contracts it talks to, what it costs, what it checks, and how to run it yourself.

Network: mainnet-beta · No devnet or testnet deployment

Overview

Duskmoth is a chat interface for private transfers on Solana. You type what you want in plain words, Duskmoth shows a readable preview, and your own wallet signs. Under the hood it uses the Privacy Cash shielded pool on mainnet, so the recipient gets paid without an on-chain link back to your wallet.

Send

Pay any Solana address in SOL or USDC without exposing your main wallet.

Shield

Park funds in your private balance now, send from it later.

Score

See how much your public wallet reveals, with plain fixes.

Duskmoth is non-custodial. It never holds keys or funds, and the AI can only propose a transaction. Every move needs your wallet's signature.

Quickstart

  1. Open the app and connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Your wallet must be on Solana mainnet.
  2. Sign once to unlock your private balance. Your wallet asks you to sign the message Privacy Money account sign in. This is free, moves nothing, and lets your browser derive the key that encrypts your private notes.
  3. Say what you want, for example send 0.5 SOL to 7xKp…sMn privately.
  4. Read the preview: amount, fees, what the recipient receives, privacy strength, and the screening result for both addresses.
  5. Confirm and sign. Your browser builds a zero-knowledge proof, the deposit goes on-chain, and a relayer pays the recipient from the pool.

Start with a small amount, such as 0.05 SOL, the first time you use it. Withdrawals have a flat fee, so very small sends are expensive (see Fees).

How a private send works

1Intent

Your sentence goes to /api/intent. Claude (or the built-in rule parser) proposes an action. The server re-checks the token, the amount, your limits, and that the recipient address appears exactly as you typed it.

2Screen

/api/screen checks valid supplied addresses using Scorechain sanctions lists. A match or an incomplete check blocks approval. This is not full AML risk screening. Transfers require your explicit confirmation and wallet approval.

3Deposit

Your wallet signs a deposit into the Privacy Cash pool. A commitment is added to the pool's Merkle tree (depth 26), and an encrypted note that only you can open is stored with it.

4Prove

Your browser generates a Groth16 proof (circuit transaction2) showing you own an unspent note, without revealing which one.

5Relay

The proof goes to the Privacy Cash relayer, through this server's /api/relayer proxy. The relayer submits the withdrawal and pays the recipient. The relayer pays the network fee, so your wallet never touches the withdrawal transaction.

For Shield now, send later, steps 4 and 5 happen whenever you choose. The longer the gap and the rounder the amount, the more deposits yours blends into.

Duskmoth limits

  • Maximum per send: 5 SOL or 500 USDC by default. Hosts can change this with MAX_SEND_SOL and MAX_SEND_USDC.
  • Supported tokens in the app: SOL and USDC. The pool supports more SPL tokens, which may be added later.
  • The relayer enforces a minimum withdrawal per token (for SOL, currently 0.01 SOL).

Sanctions screening

Duskmoth checks the recipient and any supplied sender using Scorechain's sanctions-only API. Missing senders are not claimed as checked. These checks do not enable live transfers.

GET https://sanctions.api.scorechain.com/v1/addresses/<ADDRESS>
x-api-key: <SCORECHAIN_API_KEY>
  • Scorechain returns a boolean sanctions-list match, not a risk score.
  • A match blocks approval. A no-match result is not proof that funds are safe.
  • Fails closed: invalid addresses, missing credentials, invalid provider responses, request limits, and network failures cannot pass.
  • The free service is limited to 100 lookups/hour and non-commercial use. Solana list completeness and public commercial eligibility still need confirmation.

Automated match tests use clearly labelled provider fixtures. They are not evidence of a verified live sanctioned Solana address.

Privacy score

The score reads what the public chain already says about a wallet, using the Helius Wallet API. To stop people from profiling other wallets, you must prove ownership first by signing this exact message (valid for 5 minutes, ed25519):

The score starts at 100 and is graded A to F. The share card shows only the score and grade, never the address or balance.

Helius serves identity, batch-identity and funded-by only on paid plans (free plans get 403). On a free key, those signals are skipped.

Moth Pass

Moth Pass is an optional way to unlock premium extras. You burn $DMOTH once and receive passes that are blind-signed by the server. Duskmoth works fully without it: sending, shielding, withdrawing and the privacy score never need a pass.

1Burn

Your wallet signs one public SPL burn of $DMOTH. The burn is visible on-chain to anyone.

2Blind-sign

Your browser creates random pass messages and blinds them. The server checks the burn and signs the blinded values without seeing the finalized passes (RFC 9474, suite RSABSSA-SHA384-PSS-Randomized). Your browser unblinds and verifies each signature locally.

3Spend

Each premium action spends one pass. The server can verify the signature is genuine and not yet spent, but the blind signature hides which issuance produced it.

Pre-withdraw privacy check

Before confirming a withdrawal or an immediate private send, select Check withdrawal privacy in the transaction preview. No signature, token burn, or pass is required. The check reads confirmed Solana mainnet history through Helius and reports address reuse, a return to your connected wallet, shared public activity, recent sender activity, and similar public balance changes.

Coverage is limited to the latest 20 address-history entries per address and up to 3 successful sender transactions. It is not a pool-wide analysis; token-account history, older activity, split amounts, network metadata and verified deposit age are not fully covered. Missing data is shown as unknown, never as a successful privacy result. Results are a timestamped snapshot, not an anonymity score, guarantee, sanctions check or instruction to proceed.

Your browser sends the two public addresses, token and proposed gross amount to Duskmoth. Helius receives public-history RPC queries. This feature does not persist the request or report, log addresses or amounts, or spend a pass. The optional review never replaces the required balance, fee, address and sanctions checks. AI Pack and Private Payroll are separate paid tools on /tools.

Premium tools

AI Pack turns a prompt with explicit recipient addresses and amounts into a structured multi-recipient withdrawal plan. Server validation rejects invented or missing recipients and mismatched amounts. One pass is spent only when a valid plan is successfully generated; a provider or validation failure costs nothing. Generated plans are encrypted on the server and recoverable for 7 days; expired encrypted results are cleared on subsequent ledger access while replay records remain. The recovery credential stays in your browser and is never shown. Your prompt, including public recipient addresses and amounts, is sent to Anthropic to generate the plan; never include wallet secrets.

Private Payroll prepares a SOL or USDC batch of up to 20 recipients from manual lines or CSV (recipient,amount,token), reviews fees and net amounts, then executes sequential withdrawals from your existing private balance after you confirm in your wallet. One pass is spent once per batch, after balance, all-recipient sanctions and fee checks pass, even if the batch is later stopped part-way. Duplicate recipients, mixed tokens and more than 20 recipients are rejected.

Payroll recipient lists and receipts are stored locally in this browser for recovery, and the export file contains those associations; treat it as sensitive. Nothing is scheduled, automatic or run in the background. Stop takes effect after the current payment; confirmed rows are never replayed. If a transaction outcome is uncertain, Duskmoth does not offer a resend: reconciliation only reads chain status. A stopped batch must be re-reviewed before it resumes. These tools do not guarantee anonymity.

Stored by the server

StoredWhy
Used burn transaction signatures, with an opaque digest of the blinded batch and its countSo one burn cannot be used twice, and an interrupted claim can be retried with the same blinded batch
SHA-256 hashes of spent pass messages, with random retry and grant identifiersSo one pass cannot be spent twice, and a redeem retry returns the same result
Wallet addresses, pass contents, or any link from a burn to a finalized passNot stored

Passes are bearer credits: anyone with the backup file can spend them, and clearing this browser deletes them. Back them up and keep the file private.

  • Blind signatures hide the finalized pass contents from the server. They do not hide everything: the burn is public, and timing, network and wallet metadata can still correlate activity. Burn from a different wallet than the one you send with, and wait before spending.
  • Your browser prepares and saves the blinded passes before the burn, so a burn interrupted later (closed tab, rejected claim signature, network error) can be resumed with Resume purchase instead of burning again.
  • In production, Moth Pass is disabled until the host configures the $DMOTH mint, the pass signing key and durable state. The page says which part is missing.
  • PASS_STATE_DURABLE=true declares that the signing key and ledger directory sit on genuinely persistent storage with a single writer. Setting the flag does not make ephemeral disk durable: if that storage resets, the key and ledgers are lost, unspent passes become invalid, and used burns could be claimed again. Do not set it until the storage really persists.
  • Development demo passes use a separate issuer key from the mainnet key, so they never work as mainnet passes.
  • $DMOTH is not a privacy coin. It is an ordinary SPL token with public transfers.

API reference

The Duskmoth server is a small Express app. Base path /api, JSON in and out, rate limited per IP (about 40 requests per burst). Request bodies and addresses are never logged.

GET/api/health

Liveness and which services are live.

{ "ok": true, "mode": "production", "network": "mainnet-beta", "ai": "claude",
  "screening": "scorechain", "screeningScope": "sanctions-only", "data": "helius", "liveSend": true, "demoWallet": false, "relayerProxy": true }

GET/api/config

Which services are live on this host.

{
  "ai": "claude",            // or "rules"
  "screening": "scorechain", // or "off"
  "data": "helius",          // or "public-rpc"
  "liveSend": true,          // user confirmation and wallet approval required
  "limits": { "SOL": 5, "USDC": 500 }
}

Shareable score card (1200×630 SVG). Contains no address.

POST/api/rpc

Mainnet JSON-RPC proxy, so the Helius key never reaches the browser. Up to 10 calls per batch. Only these methods are allowed:

getLatestBlockhash getAccountInfo getMultipleAccounts getBalance getTokenAccountBalance getTokenAccountsByOwner getSignatureStatuses getSignaturesForAddress getTransaction sendTransaction simulateTransaction getMinimumBalanceForRentExemption getSlot getBlockHeight getFeeForMessage getRecentPrioritizationFees

POST/api/relayer/*

Pass-through to the official Privacy Cash relayer for the paths the SDK uses (/config, /deposit, /withdraw, /utxos/*, /merkle/*). Anything else returns 404. The user's IP is not forwarded.

POST/api/waitlist

Unavailable (503) until durable storage is configured. No email is stored or reported as saved.

Self-host on mainnet

Requirements: Node.js 20+ or Docker, and a domain with HTTPS (browser wallets will not sign on plain HTTP outside localhost). The release package already contains the ZK circuit and the pre-built proving module. Full steps are in INSTALL.md.

docker compose up -d --build
docker compose exec duskmoth npm run check

Go-live checklist

  • npm run check ends with All required checks passed.
  • GET /api/health shows "mode": "production", "screening": "scorechain", "data": "helius", "liveSend": true, "demoWallet": false. Enabled does not mean a live-funds test has passed.
  • Send 0.05 SOL to a second wallet you own. Confirm the deposit and the relayer withdrawal on Solscan, and confirm no direct link between the two wallets.
  • Obtain a provider-supported sanctioned Solana test case and verify it is blocked; do not assume an old Range example is sanctioned by Scorechain.
  • Lower MAX_SEND_SOL and MAX_SEND_USDC for your first days live.
  • Check that privacy tools are legal where you and your users are.

Security model

WhoCan seeCannot do
Your walletEvery transaction before signingNot applicable: it holds your keys
Your browserYour encryption key (derived from your sign-in signature), your notes, the ZK proofNot applicable
Duskmoth serverYour sentence, the two addresses for screening, the address you score or view activity forSign, hold funds, see your private notes. It stores none of this.
Privacy Cash relayerThe withdrawal proof, recipient and amountLink the withdrawal to your deposit
Anyone on the explorerA deposit into the pool, and a withdrawal from the poolTell which deposit paid which recipient
  • API keys (Anthropic, Scorechain, Helius) live only on the server. The browser talks to Helius through /api/rpc with a method allowlist. Transaction submission is blocked while transfers are unavailable.
  • No request bodies or addresses are logged. The only thing written to disk is the waitlist email file.
  • Losing access to your wallet means losing access to your private notes too, because the encryption key is derived from your wallet's signature. Withdraw what you do not need to keep shielded.

Honest limits

  • The anonymity set is what other people deposit. If you deposit 3.217 SOL and someone receives 3.206 SOL a minute later, the amounts and timing can link you. Use round amounts and wait.
  • Privacy is not invisibility. Your deposit is still visible as a deposit into the pool.
  • Third-party dependence. Wallet features depend on Helius/RPC and sanctions checks depend on Scorechain. No failed service is replaced with invented data. Wallet-approved transfers depend on successful screening, current relayer fees, sufficient funds, proof generation and network confirmation. Provider coverage, commercial-use eligibility and live-funds verification are separate requirements.
  • The live send path is new. Test it with small amounts on mainnet before relying on it.
  • Not legal or financial advice. Privacy tools are restricted in some countries.

FAQ

Is Duskmoth a mixer?

No. It is a chat interface over a non-custodial zero-knowledge pool, with sanctions screening on both sides of every transfer. Duskmoth never takes custody of funds.

Does it work on devnet?

No. Privacy Cash and Duskmoth run on Solana mainnet-beta only. Test with small mainnet amounts instead.

Why did my wallet ask me to sign "Privacy Money account sign in"?

That signature derives the key that encrypts your private notes in the pool. It costs nothing and authorizes no transfer.

Can I get my funds out if Duskmoth disappears?

Yes. Your notes live on-chain in the Privacy Cash pool and are encrypted to your wallet. Any Privacy Cash client, including the official one, can withdraw them with the same wallet.

Why is a 0.05 SOL send so expensive?

The relayer charges a flat 0.006 SOL per withdrawal on top of 0.35%. On small amounts the flat part dominates.

What is $DMOTH?

The Duskmoth community token ticker. Duskmoth works without holding it, and no fees are paid in it.
