Night tools

Two optional tools for preparing multiple private payments. You approve the batch and unlock your private balance with your wallet. Nothing runs on a schedule or in the background, and the core app stays free.

I AI Pack

1 pass · only on a successful plan

Describe the payments in plain words, with every address and amount written out. The model structures them; it never guesses a recipient or an amount.

  • Failed or rejected generation costs nothing.
  • Your encrypted plan can be recovered for 7 days from this browser. Expired server copies are cleared on subsequent ledger access.
  • Your prompt, including public addresses and amounts, is sent to Anthropic. Never enter wallet secrets.
  • A plan is a draft. Using it moves it into Payroll review; nothing is sent.

Include explicit amounts, one token, and full Solana addresses.

II Private Payroll

1 pass per batch, not per recipient

Send a SOL or USDC batch of up to 20 recipients from your existing private balance, one relayed withdrawal at a time, after you approve the batch.

  • The pass is spent once, after balance, all-recipient sanctions and fee checks pass, even if you later stop part-way.
  • Stop takes effect after the current payment. Confirmed rows are never replayed.
  • An uncertain transaction is never resent. Reconcile reads chain status only.

III MagicBlock USDC Internal beta

Payroll 1 pass · others no pass

Private USDC sends, payroll, deposits and withdrawals through MagicBlock. Internal mainnet beta for allowlisted wallets only; not a public launch.

  • Reviews screen recipients and simulate costs. No funds or passes move.
  • Source-confirmed is not paid. A row is confirmed only after settlement is proven.
  • Nothing is resent automatically. Uncertain rows can only be checked.
  • All times are shown in UTC.

Stored in this browser

Payroll recipient lists and receipts are kept locally so an interrupted batch can be recovered. Clearing browser data removes them.

The backup file

An exported batch links your wallet to every recipient and amount. Treat it as sensitive. It never contains, and Duskmoth never asks for, private keys.

What this is not

No scheduled, recurring or automatic payments. No anonymity guarantee: amounts, timing and network metadata can still correlate activity.
